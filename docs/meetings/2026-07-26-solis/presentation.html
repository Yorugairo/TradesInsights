01
The problem

By the time a permit is public,
the GC has already picked its subs.

Everyone can buy the issued-permit list. It is the same list, at the same time, for every sub in the state. On commercial work the buyout happens during plan review — months before issuance. A lead that arrives at issuance is not early. It is late, and it looks like everyone else's.

Timing statements throughout this deck are inferences from a domain model over public dates — not facts asserted by the permit office.

The clock, per trade

Drywall and paint do not
get bought at the same moment.

Residential — after issuance
  • Drywall — 4 to 8 weeks after the permit issues
  • Paint — 6 to 12 weeks after issuance
  • Exterior paint landing Nov–Apr carries a PNW season caveat
Commercial — before issuance
  • 2 to 6 months ahead of issuance, during GMP buyout
  • Application and plan review is the only live window
  • At issuance the package is generally gone

Each opportunity carries its own window line on the correct track — “bid now”, “opens in N weeks”, or “likely closed”.
